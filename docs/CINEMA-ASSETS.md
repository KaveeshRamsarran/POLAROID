# Bellwether character assets

Created with the built-in image-generation tool for this project. No commercial game models or textures were extracted. Original PNGs are copied into the repository and included in the offline build. Geometry, UV fitting, facial blending, hands, clothing and animations are implemented locally in `src/cinema/characters.js`. Plaster aging and carpet are generated by `src/cinema/details.js`.

## Patron — `public/cinema/patron-face.png`

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: ORIGINAL front-projected facial albedo texture for a human NPC in a grounded late-1990s psychological horror game. Generate a square 1024x1024 image: a perfectly straight-on orthographic close-up of the complete head of an ordinary tired 45-year-old man, neutral closed mouth, looking directly forward, short thin dark brown hair with receding temples, natural small ears, grey-brown eyes, light neutral beige skin with subtle pores, nasolabial lines, faint stubble, slight under-eye shadows. Real human anatomy and proportions, photoreal scanned diffuse albedo appearance. Entire head from hair to chin visible. Face centered exactly horizontally; head silhouette spans roughly x=15% to85%, hair top at y=5%, chin at y=95%. Eye centers at x=37% and63%, y=40%. Mouth center x=50%, y=67%. Background plain uniform mid beige similar to skin. No shoulders, no neck, no clothing, no text or watermark. Flat extremely diffuse shadowless neutral illumination, no directional shadows, no highlights, no cinematic light, no scary expression, no corpse effects, no cartoon sculpting. This will be projected onto a shaped three-dimensional head mesh and lit in-engine, so preserve natural recognizable facial details and avoid baked lighting.

The mesh UV landmarks were fitted to the actual returned image, rather than assuming the requested eye and mouth coordinates were exact.

## Ada, manager and audience — `public/cinema/cast-faces.png`

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one original 2x2 texture atlas of FOUR different realistic human heads, diffuse albedo source for retro psychological horror videogame NPCs. Square canvas divided into EXACT four equal square quadrants with no borders/gutters/text. Each quadrant is same frontal orthographic complete head portrait on neutral beige background: top left a 36-year-old woman with dark brown hair pulled tightly back (Ada the projectionist), top right a 58-year-old broad-faced stocky man with a receding grey hairline and narrow moustache (the manager), bottom left a 65-year-old woman with short curled greying hair, bottom right a 24-year-old clean-shaven man with short brown side-parted hair. For every cell: head centered precisely, ears visible, top of hair at 4% local height, chin 98%, silhouette x15%-85%; eyes at x37% and63%, y48%; mouth centered y76%. Only heads, no necks or shoulders. Neutral closed mouths, natural skin pores, believable eyes, individual faces, late-1970s grooming. Flat neutral diffuse shadowless lighting, scanned human albedo texture appearance, no baked directional shadows or harsh highlights. Not cartoon, not clay, not sculptures, not zombies. No accessories, no captions, no frames, no watermarks. This is a technical asset used on three-dimensional head meshes lit in-engine; clear natural human detail.

The recurring patron alone uses the repaired cuff and watch; the other figures use separate atlas portraits. Both images are diffuse source artwork, not photographs of real cast members or documentary evidence.
