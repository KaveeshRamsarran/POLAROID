# Validation

- Production build: passed (`npm run build`).
- Unit tests: 12 passed, covering levels, collision, sight lines, photographic targeting, pathfinding, doors, AI memory, retreat, reaching a detected player, ritual ordering, save restoration, and objectives.
- Browser smoke test: passed; title screen, start, actual photo capture/development, film consumption, and journal; no browser runtime errors.
- Browser systems test: passed; settings persistence, wardrobe entry/exit, film pickup, zero-film emergency recovery, and save/reload.
- Browser progression test: passed; all areas connected by navigable routes, keyboard movement, door opening and collision, four real evidence captures, wrong/correct attic codes, the cellar key, all four ritual frames, blackout, final photograph, walking through the entrance, and ending. This test uses fixture checkpoints between room interactions; it is not a timed uninterrupted human playthrough.
- Browser chase/recovery test: passed; sprint noise, visible pursuit, death after the warning/grace period, and checkpoint restoration preserving evidence.
- Visual review: live menu, first-person hallway, developed photograph, study, mirror, nursery, attic, ritual chamber, hiding, and ending.
- Polish regression: all fifteen chairs point toward their tables; no overlapping parallel wall volumes; nine rays looking back from both ascending stair flights hit the enclosure; both flights remain clear with doors open; locked attic/cellar openings cannot be bypassed at their edges.
- Audio regression: OfflineAudioContext renders confirm exactly silent idle output, distinct audible wood/tile/concrete/metal footsteps, quieter crouch steps, and silent tails after each finite sound. The continuous noise loop was removed. Sound quality has not been evaluated on the user's speakers.
- Recorded audio: passed. Both supplied recordings decode in the browser and the game plays them instead of its synthesized shutter and footsteps. `Walk On Concrete - Sound Effect for editing.mp3` (5.98 s) splits into 11 separate footfalls of roughly 90 ms each, so one impact plays per stride rather than a looping walk cycle. Measured against the synthesized sounds they replace, peak output is comparable — footsteps 0.084–0.119 recorded versus 0.103–0.127 synthesized, shutter 0.237 versus 0.230 — so no volume rebalancing was needed. Crouched steps stay at roughly a third of walking level. The footstep audibility check now measures the peak and the first 150 ms, because a recorded impact is a short transient rather than the 0.48 s synthesized decay the old half-second average was calibrated for. The recordings were verified through both the Vite development server and the standalone `dist` build served by `play.mjs`. They have not been auditioned on the user's speakers.
- Updated visual review: enclosed attic stairwell looking back, dining room chair orientation and wood/plaster materials, and the Observer's revised face and silhouette. Dedicated scene fixtures use extra review lighting to reveal geometry; these are not screenshots of a live chase.
- Enlarged house: passed. Eight new areas — conservatory, library, laundry, parlour, gallery, guest room, bathroom and cold store — bring the plan to twenty, with eight new doors and nine live light sources following the player. The route check confirms every new room is reachable on foot from the entrance hall, and the full progression chain still completes. Rooms are laid out in plan rather than stacked, because a room is resolved from a single x/z lookup.
- Furniture placement: passed. Every wardrobe, couch, bookcase, sideboard, shelf unit, radiator, bed and hearth was measured back from its wall face to its own rear extent and turned to face into its room; the wardrobes that stood free in the living room, study, dining room, storage, nursery, bedrooms, attic and chamber are now flush, as is the couch, which previously faced the front elevation with three metres of open floor behind it. Clearances against door reveals and archways were checked individually. Judged from in-game screenshots of all twenty areas, not from a measured audit of every pairing.
- Door widths: reduced from a 1.7 m leaf to 1.05 m for interior doors and 1.15 m for the entrance, attic and cellar, with reveals sized to leaf plus 0.2 m and casings lapped onto the reveal so no open slot remains. Room-to-room archways narrowed from 2 m to 1.5 m. The locked attic and cellar openings still cannot be bypassed at their edges, and the test walks a player through the narrowed living room door.
- Textures: the single floor map was split into separate glazed tile and bare concrete surfaces, and boards, tile, concrete and plaster were reauthored — staggered boards with per-board tint, grain phase and knots replacing a 320-cycle sine; bevelled tiles with grout, per-tile tint and crazing; troweled concrete with pitting and hairline cracks; plaster with vertical damp runs. All four are 1024² and tile seamlessly at three world units. Anisotropic filtering raised to 16. Appearance was judged on this display only.
- Frame timings after the expansion: approximately 10.0 ms median and 10.1 ms p95, unchanged from before the new wings.

Tests used isolated headless Chrome profiles on this Windows host. A short 1280×800 medium-quality sample measured approximately 10.0 ms median and 10.1 ms p95 frame intervals. This is a local sample, not a cross-hardware performance guarantee. The requested 25–45-minute first-play duration has not been established by human playtesting.

Evidence screenshots are available in `artifacts/`. Test commands and controls are documented in `README.md`.
